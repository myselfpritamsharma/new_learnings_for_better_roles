## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Aug 28 - Dec 31, 2026) — 60% SEBI / 40% PBC

**Goal:** Build strong DSA + CS foundation that serves BOTH tracks. Start PBC-specific prep.

### SEBI Phase 1 Syllabus Must Run From Week 1

Alongside the technical foundation, schedule these recurring SEBI blocks:
- **General Awareness and financial/regulatory awareness:** 15–20 minutes, 4 days/week, with dated notes and monthly revision.
- **English:** 2 short weekly drills covering comprehension, vocabulary, grammar, and error spotting.
- **Quantitative Aptitude:** 2 short weekly drills covering arithmetic, percentages, ratios, averages, DI, and simplification.
- **Reasoning:** 2 short weekly drills covering puzzles, syllogisms, inequalities, arrangements, and logical deductions.
- **Timed practice:** One mixed sectional review every two weeks, plus two full mocks by December 31.

The final Phase 1 pattern and weightage remain notification-dependent; verify them in the official SEBI notice.

### SEBI Phase I Chapter Order

**Paper I:** Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, and General Awareness/financial-market awareness. Use formula sheets, timed sets, comprehension practice, monthly current-affairs capsules, and weekly mixed tests.

**Paper II IT:** Database and SQL → programming/data structures → algorithms → networks → operating systems → software engineering → web technologies → cybersecurity → data analytics/cloud/emerging IT. For each chapter: learn, recall without notes, solve timed MCQs, classify errors, and revisit at 1/7/21 days.

The official notification search result identifies Phase I Paper II IT content beginning with the relational model, and official SEBI results record Phase I on January 10, 2026 for that cycle. The same notice schedules the later two-paper Phase II examination for February 21, 2026. Use the next official notification to confirm the 2027 pattern, dates, and cut-offs.
